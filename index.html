About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University (SMU), working with Prof. Archan Misra on human-centered pervasive computing, wearable sensing, and intelligent ubiquitous systems. I completed my dual Ph.D.–M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Jodhpur (2020–2026) as a Prime Minister Research Fellow at the UbiSys Research Group, jointly supervised by Dr. Suchetana Chakraborty (IIT Jodhpur) and Dr. Sandip Chakraborty (IIT Kharagpur). My thesis, Wearables for Personal Well Being and Digital Health: A Pervasive Sensing Perspective, explores earable and wearable sensing for behavioural and health monitoring. My research has appeared at ACM CHI, IEEE PerCom, ACM SenSys, PACMHCI (EICS), CIKM, ICDM, IEEE/ACM CHASE, UbiComp/ISWC, and COMSNETS. I was a visiting researcher under Prof. Nirmalya Roy at the MPSC Lab, University of Maryland Baltimore County, USA.

Research vision: building intelligent, reliable, and deployable sensing systems that understand human behaviour and enable seamless interaction in everyday environments.

Research

Earable-Based Health Sensing

Developing health monitoring solutions using ear-worn IMU sensors — eating behaviour assessment (BiteSense), hydration monitoring (HydratEar), spine movement tracking (SpineSense), and covert messaging (MorsEar).

eSense IMU BLE Deep Learning

Behavioural & Focus Analysis

Inertial sensing to monitor involvement and attention during online interactions (enVolve). Detecting temporal lack of focus to identify onset of neurological disorders using earables.

Contrastive Learning LSTM CNN UbiComp

Gesture & Accessibility

Eye gesture recognition with earable inertial sensing for accessible HCI. Wrist strain detection in routine activities via inertial tokenization and LLM-based feedback (WristSense).

HCI Accessibility LLM Tokenization

AIoT & Smart Systems

Building intelligent IoT community testbeds for ambient living. Multimodal cybersickness detection in consumer VR (VectionSense). Ultrasonic defense for smart earbuds (EarFence).

IoT VR Testbed Security
