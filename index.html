About

Result-oriented, top performer, and self-starter professional with expertise in mobile computing, IoT, and operating systems.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University, working with Prof. Archan Misra. I completed my Ph.D. (PMRF) in Mobile and Pervasive Computing at the UbiSys Research Group in IIT Jodhpur under the joint supervision of Dr. Suchetana Chakraborty, IIT Jodhpur and Dr. Sandip Chakraborty, IIT Kharagpur.

My focus converges on developing ubiquitous computing solutions for health sensing, particularly behavioural sensing. My research work has featured in publications such as ACM CHI, IEEE PerCom, ACM SenSys, PACMHCI, ACM CHASE, COMSNETS, ICDM, and UbiComp/ISWC. I had the opportunity to advance my research as a visiting scholar under Prof. Nirmalya Roy at the MPSC Lab, University of Maryland Baltimore County, USA.

Research

  • Earable-Based Health Sensing

    Developing health monitoring solutions using ear-worn IMU sensors: eating behaviour assessment (BiteSense), hydration monitoring (HydratEar), spine movement tracking (SpineSense), and covert messaging (MorsEar).

    eSenseIMUBLEDeep Learning

  • Behavioural & Focus Analysis

    Inertial sensing to monitor involvement and attention during online interactions (enVolve). Detecting temporal lack of focus to identify onset of neurological disorders using earables.

    Contrastive LearningLSTMCNNUbiComp

  • Gesture & Accessibility

    Eye gesture recognition with earable inertial sensing for accessible HCI. Wrist strain detection in routine activities via inertial tokenization and LLM-based feedback (WristSense).

    HCIAccessibilityLLMTokenization

  • AIoT & Smart Systems

    Building intelligent IoT community testbeds for ambient living. Multimodal cybersickness detection in consumer VR (VectionSense). Ultrasonic defense for smart earbuds (EarFence).

    IoTVRTestbedSecurity
